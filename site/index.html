terrahour

A world clock
for the terminal

See daylight, compare time zones, and find a time that works across cities.

$ pipx install terrahour
Other ways to install →

Find a meeting time

Each city gets a bar with its working hours. The row at the bottom shows when everybody is at their desk at the same time.

~ terrahour

See clock changes coming

The DST radar lists the next clock change in every city, and warns you when the gap between you and them is about to shift by an hour.

~ terrahourpress D

Watch the stock exchanges

Swap your cities for the ten biggest exchanges, with their real trading hours and which ones are open right now.

~ terrahourpress M

And more

Alerts
Get a notification when it’s 9am in Tokyo, or when New York starts work.
Weather
See the weather next to each city, in °C or °F.
Big clock
Turn it into a large clock over the map, for a spare screen.
One line
Print all your cities on one line, for tmux or a status bar.
Themes
Pick from 13 colour themes, including a light one.
12-hour clock
Show 3:00 pm instead of 15:00.
Mouse
Click a city to select it, or drag the bars to move through the day.

Works on Linux and macOS. On Windows, run it inside WSL.
